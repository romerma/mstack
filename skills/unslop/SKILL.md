# Unslop

Write it clean as you draft it. The cleanup-afterwards pass has been measured to fail, so the
rule is not "fix the bad sentence", it is **do not generate it**.

## Content

1. **No puffery.** Not "powerful", "seamless", "robust", "comprehensive". Say what it does.
2. **No name-dropping** to borrow credibility a claim has not earned.
3. **No superficial -ing clauses.** "...enabling teams to move faster" is a sentence that ends
   without saying anything.
4. **No vague attribution.** "It is widely considered" means you do not have a source.
5. **No formulaic challenge framing.** "But there is a catch." "Here is the thing."
6. **Every sentence earns its place.** If it could appear unchanged in another project's docs,
   it says nothing about this one. Cut it.

## Language

7. **Ban the tell vocabulary**: delve, leverage, utilize, robust, seamless, crucial, pivotal,
   testament, landscape, realm, tapestry, underscore, myriad, plethora, navigate, unlock.
8. **Say "is".** Not "serves as", "acts as", "functions as", "represents".
9. **No "not just X, but Y."** Say Y.
10. **No rule of three by reflex.** Three items because there are three, not for the cadence.
11. **No synonym cycling.** The same thing keeps the same name. Renaming it mid-paragraph makes
    the reader wonder whether it changed.
12. **No false ranges.** "From X to Y" only when there is a real spectrum.

## Style

13. **No em dashes.** If a sentence needs one, it is two sentences.
14. **Colons introduce, they do not join.**
15. **Bold marks a term, not emphasis.** A paragraph with four bold phrases has none.
16. **No inline-header lists.** A bullet is a sentence, not a label plus a dash plus a clause.
17. **Sentence case for headings.**
18. **No decorative emoji.** A status marker in a table is fine.
19. **Straight quotes.**

## Communication

20. **No chatbot framing.** Not "Great question", not "I hope this helps".
21. **No cutoff disclaimers** unless the recency actually affects the answer.
22. **No sycophancy.** Agreement is not the default. A recommendation is a judgment, not a
    validation, and "no" is an acceptable answer.

## Filler

23. **Cut hedges** that carry no probability: "arguably", "it could be said", "in some sense".
24. **Cut throat-clearing.** Start at the first sentence that carries information.
25. **Cut the summary of what you just said**, unless the thing was long enough to need one.

## Plain speech

26. **Say what it does, not how it feels.** "Fast" is a feeling; "12ms at p95" is a fact.
27. **Restate the sentence as an instruction, a fact, or a number.** If you cannot, cut it.
28. **Split dense sentences.** One clause, one idea.
29. **Active voice**, unless the actor genuinely does not matter.
30. **Cut adverbs** that prop up a weak verb.
31. **Prefer the plain word.** "Use", not "utilize". "Help", not "facilitate".

## Adding soul

Removing patterns is half the job. Sterile, voiceless prose is just as recognisable. A specific
detail, a real number, an honest admission of what you do not know, a sentence with a shape to
it: those are what make writing sound like a person wrote it, and none of them are on the list
above.
